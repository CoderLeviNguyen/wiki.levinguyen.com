## Directly checked records
1. **Nuts cognition meta-analysis (2024)** — PMID 39296505, DOI 10.3389/fnut.2024.1463801, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39296505/ . Five randomized trials (928 adults); pooled cognitive outcome SMD 0.27, 95% CI -0.65 to 1.19, p=0.57. Overall benefit **not statistically established**. Relevant as contextual contradictory evidence to generalized nut/cognition claims (BF-01, BF-02, BF-07, BF-09, BF-11); it does not separately refute a specific species/outcome.
2. **Nut systematic review (2021)** — PMID 33330927, DOI 10.1093/advances/nmaa153, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33330927/ . Twenty-two studies (43,793 people); results heterogeneous; walnut studies more favorable, but observational evidence and mixed outcomes cannot prove generalized improvement. BF-01, BF-02.
3. **Mixed tree nut randomized crossover (2023)** — PMID 36202391, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36202391/ . Seventy-nine healthy adults aged 18–49, 30 g mixed nuts/day for four weeks vs placebo; picture-recognition accuracy and response speed improved; not attributable to an individual nut. NOTE: Search index also exposed an alternate record PMID 37230675 for a closely titled article; use DOI and journal details before deduplication.
4. **Pistachio uncontrolled pilot (2024)** — PMID 38943918, DOI 10.1016/j.foodchem.2024.140211, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38943918/ . Twenty-eight g/day for 28 days, overweight young adults; improved selective/sustained attention tests; absence of a control group prevents strong causal inference. BF-09 preliminary only, BF-10 alertness not directly established.
5. **Dates randomized crossover (2026)** — PMID 42559168, DOI 10.3389/fnut.2026.1733218, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42559168/ . Thirty-five healthy young adults, date cultivars compared with sugar/fibre-matched placebo; no significant overall cognitive indices; one cultivar increased alertness and decreased calmness, not a general effect. Does not measure fatigue reduction or demonstrate functional energy benefit. BF-05/BF-06 unsupported by this study.
6. **Raisins randomized trial (2023)** — PMID 37375715, DOI 10.3390/nu15122811, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37375715/ . Eighty older adults >70, 50 g/day for six months; improvements on some cognition measures, including MOCA; no direct calmness endpoint. BF-03 indirect; BF-04 not established.
7. **Almond RCT positive for some domains (2026)** — PMID 41850683, DOI 10.1016/j.tjnut.2026.101484, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41850683/ . Open-label trial of 60 adults with prediabetes over 24 weeks; executive function and processing speed improved. Funding: Almond Board of California. Does not establish memory or learning benefit universally. BF-07/BF-08 endpoint mismatch.
8. **Almond RCT null (2026)** — PMID 41691115, DOI 10.1038/s41430-026-01705-6, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41691115/ . Crossover study, 50 g/day for five months, adults with prediabetes; no significant measured cognitive benefit. Contrasts with source 7.
